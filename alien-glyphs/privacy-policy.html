Alien Glyphs: Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

In short: Alien Glyphs has no accounts and doesn't collect your personal information itself. Your progress stays on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Who we are

Alien Glyphs is a puzzle game for Android, made by Mohamed Hassan ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings in a file on your own device: your stars and best move counts, which levels and lessons you've completed, how many hints you have left, your Rotor Rush best score, and your sound, music and display settings. This file is never sent to us. It's deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Some ads are optional rewarded ads you choose to watch (for extra hints, or more time in Rotor Rush). Others appear between levels or after a Rotor Rush run. To show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, and an approximate location worked out from it;
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown and whether they were tapped;
  • diagnostic information about how the ads performed.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the Privacy option in the game's menu. You can also reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android device settings.

We don't receive the information Google collects. We only see combined reports, such as how many ads were shown and how much they earned. To learn how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • The game has no accounts, chat or social features.

Children

Alien Glyphs is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads in the game are limited to content rated suitable for teens.

Your rights

Because we don't hold personal information about you, requests about the information collected for ads are handled by Google (see the links above). If you have any questions about this policy or your privacy, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game uses information, we'll update this page and its effective date.
