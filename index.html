Alien Glyphs

A puzzle game about lining up linked rings of Martian glyphs.

About the game

Turn each ring until the glyph under the gold line matches its key. Rings drag each other along, some are sealed shut, and some machines flip their links mid-solve. Forty-eight handcrafted machines, short lessons on the maths behind them, and Rotor Rush, a 60-second arcade mode.
